# Future Features

1. **AI-powered ride matching** – Match rides to users based on preferences, route, and past behavior
2. **Natural language search** – Search with phrases like "rides to Boston next weekend under $30"
3. **In-app payments** – Pay for seats via Stripe or PayPal; split fare, tips, refunds
4. **In-app chat** – Direct messaging between driver and riders for coordination
5. **Map view & live location** – See route, pickup points, and real-time ETA
6. **Push notifications** – Alerts for new requests, acceptances, and ride reminders
7. **Recurring rides** – Create daily or weekly commutes in one go
8. **User verification** – ID and background checks for drivers; verification badges
9. **Saved searches & alerts** – Save filters and get notified when matching rides appear
10. **Social login** – Sign in with Google, Facebook, or Apple
